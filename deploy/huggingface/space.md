# BrandShield AI

Real-time brand risk and sentiment intelligence: it collects real comments from YouTube,
Instagram, news and app stores, classifies them with a multilingual DistilBERT model, breaks the
opinions down by aspect (delivery, service, price, food quality…), detects crises with an
Isolation Forest, forecasts sentiment with Prophet, and raises alerts.

Final year project — K.K. Wagh Institute of Engineering Education & Research, Nashik.
